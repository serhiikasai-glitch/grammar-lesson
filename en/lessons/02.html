No article

In the previous lesson, we learned about articles and how to use the indefinite article a. Now learn the following cases where no article is used.

Before names and forms of address

This is   Pete.
This is   mister Brown.

Do not use an article before names or forms of address such as mister Mr or missis Mrs.

Before uncountable nouns

This is   milk.

Do not use a/an before nouns referring to things that are not counted as individual objects, for example, water, milk, sugar and so on

This is sugar. - This is sugar.
This is juice. - This is juice.

Learn the question words who who and what what, and the questions that use them.
