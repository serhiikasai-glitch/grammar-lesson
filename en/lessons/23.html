Verbs in Present Simple: special cases

In the previous lesson, we learned that in Present Simple verbs add the ending -s in the third-person singular. In this lesson, we will look at special cases of Present Simple, including when to add -es.

So the ending -es is used in the following cases:

— if the verb ends in -ss, -ch, -sh, -x or the vowel -o: I wash => he washes, I go => he goes

— if the verb ends in -y preceded by a consonant; the letter y changes to i: I carry => he carries

Notice that if the verb ends in -y preceded by a vowel, add the ending -s: I play => he plays

The table below shows the cases where the third-person singular Present Simple adds the ending -es.
