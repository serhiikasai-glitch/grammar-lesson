Questions with the pattern there is/there are

Question forms

In questions with there is/there are verb is/are goes before there.

Is there a book on the table?
Is there a book on the table?
Are there pencils on the table?
Are there pencils on the table?

Short answers

In short answers to questions with there is/there are pay attention to the form of the verb to be. Negative answers usually use contracted forms of to be.

Positive answers look like this:

Is there ...? - Yes, there is.
Are there ...? - Yes, there are.

Negative answers look like this:

Is there ...? - No, there isn't.
Are there ...? - No, there aren't.
