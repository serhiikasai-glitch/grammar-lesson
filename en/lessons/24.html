Questions in Present Simple

To make questions in Present Simple, and in short answers, use the auxiliary verb do. Remember that in the third-person singular this auxiliary is does.

Question forms

In a question, the auxiliary goes before the subject.

SingularPlurals
Do I ?Do we?
Do you?Do you?
Does he/she/it?Do they?

Here are examples of questions in Present Simple.

Do you like pizza?
Do you like pizza?
Does he speak English?
Does he speak English?

Short answers

Short answers to questions in Present Simple look like this:

QuestionYESNO
Do you ...?Yes, I/we do. No, I/we don't.
Does he/she/it ...?Yes, he/she/it does. No, he/she/it doesn't.
Do they ...?Yes, they do. No, they don't.
Do I/we ...?Yes, you do. No, you don't.
