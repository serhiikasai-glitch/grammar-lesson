Negatives with the verb to be

Negative sentences with to be are simple to make: after the verb to be put the negative word not. This gives us the following forms:

I am notwe are not
you are notyou are not
he
she  is not
it 		they are not

Examples of the verb to be in the negative form:

I am not from Canada. — I am not from Canada.
He is not from Ukraine. — He is not from Ukraine
We are not from the USA. - We are not from the USA.

In conversation, the negative forms of to be can be contracted: am not => 'm not, is not => isn't, are => aren't.

I'm not from Canada. — I am not from Canada.
He isn't from Ukraine. — He is not from Ukraine
We aren't from the USA. - We are not from the USA.
