The definite article the

In earlier lessons, we met the indefinite article. English also has the definite article the, which developed historically from the demonstrative that and has a related meaning.

The definite article is used with singular and plural nouns. Use it for a known or specific person or thing, for example, something already mentioned or clear from the situation.

Look at these sentences:

This is a box. The box is brown.

The first sentence introduces a thing, so it uses a. The second sentence uses the definite article the because the thing is already known.



Using articles

In some situations, the article the can point to a thing in a similar way to this/that, especially when the thing is nearby. For example:

The mouse is small. — The mouse is small.

This sentence refers to an animal the speaker can see, so the meaning is similar to “This mouse is small”.

For now, remember that after the patterns This is / That is use a/an for a new singular countable noun. For plurals, after These are and Those are do not use a/an before the noun.

This is a green apple. — This is a green apple.
That is a mouse. - This is a mouse.
These are apples - These are apples.
