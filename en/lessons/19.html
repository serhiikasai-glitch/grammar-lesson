Questions with the pattern have got

To make a question with have got put the verb to have before the subject:

SingularPlurals
Have I got? Have we got?
Have you got? Have you got?
Has he/she/it got? Have they got?

Short answers

In short answers to questions with have got use only the forms of to have. Negative answers usually use contractions.

Short answers to questions with have got look like this:

QuestionYESNO
Have you got?Yes, I/we have. No, I/we haven't.
Has he/she/it got?Yes, he/she/it has. No, he/she/it hasn't.
Have they got?Yes, they have. No, they haven't.
Have I/we got?Yes, you have. No, you haven't.
