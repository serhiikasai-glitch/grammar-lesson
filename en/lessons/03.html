Plurals

When a word names one thing, person or animal, for example, table, boy, it is singular. Use the plural for two or more things, for example, tables, boys and so on

The main rule

a lamp lamps
an apple apples

To make most nouns plural, add the ending -s.

Special cases

a glass glasses
a peach peaches
a tomato tomatoes
a baby babies

When making plurals, notice the following cases.

  • For nouns ending in -s, -ss, -x, -sh, -ch, -o add -es.


    a bus
    buses

    a box
    boxes

    a dish
    dishes

  • For nouns ending in consonant + y, the ending -y changes to -ies. If the final y is preceded by a vowel, follow the usual rule and add -s.


    a strawberry
    strawberries

    a monkey
    monkeys

The indefinite article and plurals

Notice that the indefinite article a/an is not used with plural nouns.


a banana
bananas



Patterns This is and These are

Pattern this is is used only with singular nouns. For plural nouns, use the pattern these are, which also introduces things: This is… .

This is a girl.
These are girls.
