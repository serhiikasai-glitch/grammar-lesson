Plurals. Exceptions

Some English nouns have irregular plurals. Learn these forms rather than adding the usual ending.

a man men
a woman women
a child children
a foot feet
a tooth teeth
a mouse mice
a goose geese

Words fish fish and sheep sheep have the same singular and plural form.

a fish fish
a sheep sheep

In some nouns, the ending -f(e) changes to -ves.

a leaf leaves
a knife knives
