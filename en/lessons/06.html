English personal pronouns

Every language has words that refer to people or things without naming them. Examples include I, you, he, she and so on. These words are called personal pronouns.

Personal pronouns in English

I - I we - we
you - you you - you
he - he they - they
she - she
it - it

The pronoun it

Pronoun it can refer to things and animals. In languages with grammatical gender, its translation may vary: he/she/it (depending on the noun’s grammatical gender)

it - he (ball)
it - she (cat)
it - it (apple)
