Demonstratives this and that

Meaning

Pronoun this points to a nearby thing or person.

Pronoun that points to a thing or person farther away.

Forms

This and that have different singular and plural forms

SingularPlurals
this these
that those

Use

Pronouns this and that can be used with nouns or on their own.

If this or that are used with a noun, choose the singular or plural form to match it. Do not add an article before the noun.

this boy — this boy
that girl — that girl
these apples — these apples
those oranges — those oranges

This and that can also stand alone. When they are the subject in patterns such as This is, use a singular or plural form to match the noun. Other languages may translate both forms with the same word.

This is an apple. - This is an apple.
That is a lamp. - This is a lamp.
These are apples - These are apples.
Those are lamps. - These are lamps.
