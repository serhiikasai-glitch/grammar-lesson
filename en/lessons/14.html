Prepositions of place

Prepositions of place describe where people or things are and help answer the question where?. Examples of their equivalents in other languages include in, on, before, under and so on

Here are some common English prepositions:

on - on
in - in
near - near
under - under
above - above

English sentences with prepositions of place often use the verb to be to be. In other languages, this verb may be translated as to be meaning to be located, to lie, to stand, to hang and so on

The table is in the room.
Table is in the room.
The book is on the table
Book is on the table.
The chair is near the desk.
Chair is near the table.
